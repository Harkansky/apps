Pop Template is a small offline game for iPhone and Android.

## Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email [arkansky@proton.me](mailto:arkansky@proton.me?subject=Pop Template) and mention your device model.

**Restoring purchases:** open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

**Getting your progress back on a new or reset device:** on iPhone and iPad, sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on; your progress comes back on its own. On Android, restore your Google backup when setting up the phone, then reinstall the app.

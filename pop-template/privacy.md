_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Pop Template is made by Grégoire Wartner ("I"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and I run no server: your data is never sent to me. The app works offline.

## Where your data is saved

Your progress, settings and purchased items are saved on your device. They also follow your own Apple or Google account, without any sign-in in the app:

- **iPhone and iPad:** your progress is synced through your iCloud account to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, when iCloud is turned on.
- **Android:** the app's data is included in your device's Google backup, when backup is turned on, and comes back when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone from that backup.

These copies are stored by Apple or Google in your account, under their privacy policies. I can't see or access them. You can stop them in your device's iCloud or backup settings; the app keeps working without them.

Purchases can always be restored from the Settings screen of the app.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I never see your payment details. I receive only anonymous sales reports from the stores. Their privacy policies apply to those transactions.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this changes (for example if the app adds a feature that needs data), this page will be updated before the new version is released.
